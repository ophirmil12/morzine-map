Morzine interactive ski map, test version. Last updated 25 November 2026.

This map is a test version for personal testing by a small group of people. It is not a finished product and not an official service. By using it you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, please do not use it.

1. A test version

The map is being built and tested. Features may change, stop working or be removed at any time, without notice. It is offered free of charge, as it is and as available.

2. Do not rely on it for safety

Piste and lift lines, difficulty colours, descriptions, lengths, altitudes, ride times and opening status may be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Always follow the official piste maps, the signs on the slopes and the instructions of the ski patrol and lift staff. Skiing and moving in the mountains carry risks, and you are responsible for your own choices and safety.

The live opening status of runs and lifts comes from a third-party service. It can be late or wrong, and it may be missing.

3. Accessibility

Because this is a test version for personal testing, it has not been assessed against accessibility standards and it does not offer accessibility features at this stage. It may be hard or impossible to use with a screen reader, keyboard-only navigation or other assistive technology. Accessibility will be reviewed before any public release.

4. Location sharing

  • Sharing your position is voluntary. You can switch it off at any time in the legend.
  • While it is on and the map is open, your position is sent every few minutes, between 6:00 and 20:00 French time, and shown on the map to other people who share. They also see the name you choose.
  • For safety, positions are kept for up to 2 days so they can help in an emergency, then deleted. They are not shown in the app and are not used for anything else.
  • This is not an emergency or rescue service. In an emergency call 112, or the ski patrol.

5. Your data

The map uses no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. Your settings (for example the map style and your sharing choice) are saved only on your own device. Your position leaves your device only when location sharing is on, as described above.
